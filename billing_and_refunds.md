# Billing and Refunds

## Refund policy
Acme Cloud offers a full refund within 30 days of your first paid subscription purchase. To request a refund, go to Settings > Billing > Request refund, or email billing@acmecloud.example with your invoice number. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Renewals and cancellation
Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period. To cancel, open Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the paid period. Renewal payments are not refundable after the 30-day window.

## Payment methods and invoices
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Invoices are emailed after every payment and can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > Invoices. To update your card, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry after 3 days and again after 7 days. Your account enters read-only mode after the final failed attempt. Update your payment method to restore full access immediately.
