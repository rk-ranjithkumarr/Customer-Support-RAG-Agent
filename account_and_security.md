# Account and Security

## Reset your password
Click Forgot password on the login page and enter your email. A reset link arrives within 5 minutes and stays valid for 60 minutes. Check your spam folder if you do not see it. If you signed in with Google or GitHub, reset your password with that provider instead.

## Two-factor authentication
Enable two-factor authentication in Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication. We support authenticator apps and hardware security keys. Save your 10 recovery codes somewhere safe. If you lose your device and your codes, contact support to verify your identity.

## Change your email
Go to Settings > Profile > Email and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address. Your email changes only after you click that link.

## Delete your account
Go to Settings > Profile > Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes all projects after a 14 day grace period. Cancel your subscription first to avoid a final charge.

## Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or reset your password to unlock it immediately.
