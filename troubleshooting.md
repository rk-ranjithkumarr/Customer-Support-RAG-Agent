# Troubleshooting

## Files will not upload
Uploads fail when a file exceeds 500 MB or when your storage quota is full. Check Settings > Usage to see your quota. Try a different browser or disable extensions that block network requests. Corporate firewalls may block uploads, so ask your IT team to allow acmecloud.example.

## Slow performance
Clear your browser cache, then check the status page at status.acmecloud.example for incidents. Projects with more than 10000 items load faster when you use filters or the archive feature.

## Cannot log in with SSO
SSO is available on the Business plan. Make sure your identity provider has the correct ACS URL and that your email domain is verified in Settings > Security > SSO. Contact your workspace admin if the problem continues.

## Support hours
Email support is available 24 hours a day on paid plans. Priority support for Business customers responds within 4 hours. Free plan users can use the community forum.
