# Plans and Pricing

## Available plans
Free plan: 1 project, 1 GB storage, community support. Pro plan: 19 USD per user per month, unlimited projects, 100 GB storage, email support with a 24 hour response time. Business plan: 49 USD per user per month, SSO, audit logs, 1 TB storage, priority support with a 4 hour response time.

## Annual discount
Annual billing saves 20 percent compared to monthly billing on the Pro and Business plans. You can switch from monthly to annual in Settings > Billing > Change billing cycle. The remaining monthly balance is credited toward the annual price.

## Upgrading and downgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the current period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. If your usage exceeds the lower plan limits, you must reduce storage before the downgrade completes.

## Student and nonprofit discounts
Verified students and registered nonprofits receive 50 percent off the Pro plan. Send proof of eligibility to billing@acmecloud.example.
